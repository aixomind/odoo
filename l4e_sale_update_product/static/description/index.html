Stop Canceling Confirmed Orders Just to Replace a Product

In standard Odoo, once a Sales Order is confirmed, the product field becomes permanently read-only. If a customer requests a substitute product or an alternative variant, your team is forced into a painful administrative loop: canceling delivery orders, canceling invoices, resetting the sales order to draft, manually re-entering custom agreed prices, and re-confirming.

Sale Update Product completely solves this hassle. It provides an intuitive 1-click update wizard directly from the order lines or action menu. You can substitute any product while guaranteeing that unit prices, quantities, taxes, and customer discounts remain 100% untouched.

Comprehensive Feature List

Everything you need to modify confirmed order items safely without jeopardizing commercial pricing or inventory accuracy.

Update on Confirmed Orders

Update items on orders in Confirmed (Sale) status with zero need to unlock, reset to draft, or re-approve the entire sales document.

Zero Price & Qty Discrepancy

Preserves unit prices, quantities, taxes, and discounts exactly as originally agreed. Avoids unintentional pricelist recalculations.

Stock & Delivery Synchronization

Automatically unreserves the old product on open stock moves, assigns the new product, and re-reserves warehouse inventory seamlessly.

Customer Invoice Sync

Updates corresponding customer invoice lines linked to the sales order line so your accounting records match warehouse reality.

Single & Bulk Update Modes

Swap individual lines via a dedicated line-level exchange button, or update multiple lines at once via the order's Action Menu.

Tamper-Proof Chatter Audit Trail

Every product swap automatically logs a clean, formatted note in the sales order chatter with timestamps, user, old product, and new product.

How It Works

A step-by-step visual walkthrough of the module in action.

1 Line-Level "Update Product" Action

On confirmed Sales Orders, each eligible undelivered order line features a dedicated exchange button. Simply click the button directly on the order line to trigger the product replacement without resetting the order to draft.

2 Select Replacement Product (Single-Line Wizard)

A clean popup modal appears displaying the current product. Select the replacement product from the dropdown. The agreed unit price, quantity, and taxes remain fully protected.

3 Multi-Line Bulk Update Wizard (Via Action Menu)

Need to update multiple order items at once? Open the wizard from the order action menu to review all lines in a clear table and assign new products in a single bulk operation.
