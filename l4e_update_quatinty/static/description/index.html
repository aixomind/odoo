The Standard Odoo Limitation

  • Odoo restricts changing product tracking to Lots/Serials once inventory transactions exist.
  • Warehouse managers are forced to scrap or adjust existing stock out, then re-import it with lot numbers.
  • Distorts accounting valuation, creates extra duplicate picking moves, and disrupts open orders.
  • Very time-consuming and error-prone when managing hundreds of untracked serial items.
✓

The L4E Solution

  • Converts stock in-place: Directly modifies warehouse stock quants to bind them to lots/serials.
  • Zero stock duplication: Total on-hand quantity remains exactly the same before and after.
  • Preserves audit integrity: Original packages, locations, and in-dates are maintained seamlessly.
  • Automatic move re-assignment: Safely unreserves open moves and re-reserves them with new serials.

How It Works Step-by-Step

See how easy it is to manage serial and lot assignments in real production environments.

STEP 1

Smart Button on Product Form

A dedicated Update Lot/Serial smart button appears on Storable Products, displaying the exact quantity of on-hand inventory needing lot or serial assignment.

STEP 2

Intuitive & Safe Conversion Wizard

Clicking the smart button launches the wizard. It highlights that no duplicate stock will be created and provides real-time indicators for Available to Assign, Assigned Quantity, and Remaining Quantity.

STEP 3

One-Click Bulk Serial Auto-Generation

Enter your initial serial number pattern (e.g. 0000001) and count. Clicking Generate Serials automatically creates the sequential entries with quantity 1.0 each.

STEP 4

Batch Import from Excel (.xlsx) or CSV

Have manufacturer barcodes or a supplier manifest? Simply select your Excel or CSV file and click Import.

(a) Upload Excel / CSV file directly inside the wizard

(b) Lines populated automatically with quantities and locations

STEP 5

Lot to Unique Serial Number Conversion

Need to split an existing batch Lot into individual serialized products? Select the source lot in the Convert From dropdown to break down the multi-unit batch into unique serials.

(a) Select the existing Lot to convert

(b) Generate individual serials for the batch quantity

RESULT

Verified Inventory Quants (Zero Duplication)

Checking the stock location confirms that each unit has been directly assigned its unique serial number. Total on-hand quantity remains exact with no extra moves or double counts!

AUDIT

Full Audit Trail in Product Chatter

Every update writes a structured, formatted log in the product chatter, recording the converted source (Untracked or Lot), the quantity updated, and all assigned numbers for complete traceability.
