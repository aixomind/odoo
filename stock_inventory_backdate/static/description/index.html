Inventory Adjustment Backdating & Historical Counts

Move validated inventory adjustments and linked journal entries to the correct past period in a single atomic transaction - or import historical counting sheets with accurate past valuation.

Validating Adjustments Stamps "Today" - Distorting Historical Valuation Reports

In native Odoo, validating an inventory adjustment immediately hardcodes the current date and time on stock moves, move lines, and accounting journal entries with no supported way to correct it afterwards.

If a physical stock count happened last month but is entered today, your financial and stock valuation reports show the variance in the wrong accounting period.

Inventory Adjustment Backdating solves this by allowing you to safely move validated adjustments - along with stock moves, move lines, journal entries, and analytic lines - to any chosen past date in one atomic transaction, while maintaining a full audit log.

How It Works

A visual walkthrough of the backdating workflow and past counts management.

1 Filter and Load Validated Adjustments

Navigate to Inventory ➔ Date Corrections ➔ Backdate Inventory Adjustments. Set your validation date range, optional product, location, or reference filter, and click Load Adjustments. All matching adjustments appear with current dates, quantities, and linked journal entries.

2 Choose New Target Date & Rewrite Settings

Select the target date and time. Configure which linked models to update - Stock Move Lines, Journal Entries, Analytic Lines, and Chatter log notes. Click Apply to execute all updates in one atomic database transaction.

3 Manage Historical Counting Sheets

In Inventory ➔ Date Corrections ➔ Past Inventory Counts, import or create physical count sheets. Rows are evaluated in oldest-first order against what stock actually stood at on that date, calculating exact variance.

4 Apply Count with Historical Valuation & Journal Link

Applying a row posts the stock move and creates the accounting entry directly into the counted period. The form view displays full breakdown metrics including quantity before, difference, valuation value, and a direct clickable link to the created Journal Entry.

5 Historical Period-Aligned Accounting Journal Entries

Clicking the generated Journal Entry link opens the entry directly. Notice that the journal entry date, period sequence number, and valuation debit/credit line balances match the historical count date and cost price.

6 Permanent Read-Only Audit History

Under Inventory ➔ Date Corrections ➔ Backdating History, view full read-only audit records. Tracks previous vs new dates, previous vs new accounting dates, journal entry numbers, user, and execution timestamps.
