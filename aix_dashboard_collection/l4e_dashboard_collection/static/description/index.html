How It Works

A step-by-step visual walkthrough of the module in action.

1 One Tile on Your Odoo Home Screen

Once installed, a new Dashboards app tile appears right on your Odoo home screen alongside Sales, Inventory, and Accounting no separate portal or external tool needed.

2 Enable the Dashboards You Need

Go to Settings > Dashboard Collection and turn on CRM, Sales, Inventory, Account, and/or AMC Dashboard under Dashboard Visibility. Only administrators can toggle this, and the "Dashboards" menu updates instantly for every user.

3 Track Leads & Pipeline Performance on the CRM Dashboard

The CRM Dashboard gives an executive overview of Total Leads, Won Revenue, Lost Revenue, Quotation Pending, Overdue Activities, and Activities Due Today with live filters by Year, Sales Team, Salesperson, and Date Range.

Analyze the Sales Team Performance horizontal bar chart and Recent Won Orders side-by-side, plus drill down into Top Salespersons, Top Customers, and Upcoming Activities.

4 Check Sales Performance at a Glance

The Sales Dashboard shows Total Sale Orders, To Invoice, Partially Invoiced, Fully Invoiced, and Quotations with live order counts and amounts, plus quick year and date-range filters so you can jump between periods in one click.

5 Track Stock Valuation on the Inventory Dashboard

See Total Inventory Value, Total Products, Stock Moves, Low Stock Products, and In/Out movement all with a live % change vs. last month, plus an Inventory Value Over Time trend chart and an Inventory by Product Category breakdown. Filter by product or by This Month / Last 30 Days / This Quarter / This Year, or a custom date range.

Scroll further for Top 5 Products by Value, Stock by Location, and Top 5 Product Moves in and out, each with a "view all" shortcut to the full record.

6 Review Financial Ratios by Category

The Account Dashboard opens with Customer Invoices, Salaries, and Vendor Bills summary cards Not Paid, In Payment, Paid, Partially Paid, Reversed, Blocked, Payment Released, and Total each filterable by customer/vendor/employee and date.

Scroll down and pick a category from the Financial Category side menu Profitability, Liquidity, Solvency, Efficiency, Valuation, Cash Flow, or Return Metrics to see ready-made ratios like Revenue Growth Rate, Gross Profit Margin, Net Profit Margin, and EBITDA, each with its formula shown and compared automatically against the previous period.

7 Stay on Top of AMC & Service Contracts

Filter the full AMC Management Dashboard by employee, customer, or date range to see Projects, Services, Completed, Ongoing, Not Started, and Overdue counts, a Service Status Distribution donut chart, and a Services Scheduled Per Month bar chart.

The AMC Dashboard starts with an Upcoming Scheduled Services list Customer, AMC Reference, Scheduled Date, Product/Asset, assigned Employee, and Status (Done, Overdue, New) so nothing slips through the cracks.
